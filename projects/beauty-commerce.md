**Independent work**

Independently developed the backend and administration panel for a personal marketplace platform combining beauty service booking and product commerce.

Implemented multi-role account workflows for customers, professionals, salon owners, and academy owners, together with service discovery, scheduling, booking lifecycle management, payments, wallet transactions, and commission allocation.

Built commerce workflows for product catalogs, carts, checkout, inventory reservations, payment receipt review, and order fulfillment. Added transaction handling, idempotency checks, and status histories to support consistent processing and traceability.

Developed a Razor Pages administration interface for operational management and review workflows. Implemented real-time booking and messaging updates with SignalR, Redis-backed session and cache components, and scheduled background processing. Added xUnit tests covering account access, wallet operations, inventory reservations, and checkout behavior.

**Core technologies:** C#, .NET, ASP.NET Core, Entity Framework Core, SQL Server, MediatR/CQRS, Redis, SignalR, Razor Pages, Quartz.NET, xUnit
