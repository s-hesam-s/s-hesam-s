**SabaTech · Internal prototypes**

Designed and built two internal browser-based training prototypes that translate crowdfunding workflows into interactive learning experiences.

Implemented scenario and decision logic, progress persistence, and a branching adventure with navigation and evidence collection. Used JavaScript, Canvas, and Three.js for interaction and rendering, supported by a local Node.js server and automated logic tests.

Developed the prototypes for internal demonstration and testing, combining financial domain knowledge with full-stack development and interactive experience design.

**Core technologies:** JavaScript, Canvas, Three.js, Node.js
